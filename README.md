# Ila Firdawsi for Android

**[Download the app (APK)](https://github.com/hacker123as/ila-firdawsi-app/raw/refs/heads/main/ila-firdawsi.apk)**

Prayer, Qur'an and daily spiritual rhythm, undisturbed. The app is the live
[ila-firadawsi-app.vercel.app](https://ila-firadawsi-app.vercel.app/), so it is always up to date, with a prayer
lock, Google sign-in and your own theme.

## Installing

1. Open the download link on your Android phone (Android 7 or newer).
2. When asked, allow your browser or Files app to install apps.
3. Open **Ila Firdawsi** and sign in with the same account as on the website.

Updates install over the top of the old app, and you stay signed in. The app tells you when a new
version is ready.
